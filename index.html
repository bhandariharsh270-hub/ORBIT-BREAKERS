The Rocket Equation in Action:

Every kilogram of dry mass added to your payload increases the propellant mass required exponentially based on your target orbit's Delta-V.

Δv = Isp · 9.80665 · ln(m_wet / m_dry)

Launch Vehicle Envelopes:

If your spacecraft wet mass exceeds the rocket's payload capacity to the destination, launch is strictly prohibited by NASA Range Safety.
